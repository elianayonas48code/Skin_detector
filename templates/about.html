ABOUT NEBIR AI

About NEBIR AI

NEBIR AI is an artificial intelligence project designed to analyze skin images and provide predictions across seven skin-disease categories.

Our Mission

Our mission is to explore how artificial intelligence and computer vision can support healthcare education and research.

What NEBIR AI Does

NEBIR AI analyzes an uploaded skin image using an artificial intelligence image-classification model. The system then provides a predicted category and confidence information.

Seven Skin Categories

The model works with seven categories: Actinic Keratoses, Basal Cell Carcinoma, Benign Keratosis, Dermatofibroma, Melanoma, Melanocytic Nevi, and Vascular Lesion.

Our Purpose

NEBIR AI is developed as an educational and research prototype demonstrating the use of machine learning and computer vision in healthcare.

!

Important Medical Notice

NEBIR AI provides an AI-based prediction for educational and research purposes. It is not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.
